Academic Notes on Complexity Metrics:
1. FLOPs vs MACs: 1 Multiply-Accumulate (MAC) corresponds to ~2 Floating Point Operations (FLOPs).
2. Memory vs Compute: Dense Linear layers contribute the majority of parameters, but Conv layers dominate FLOPs.
3. Reference: He et al. (CVPR 2016), Howard et al. (MobileNets 2017).
